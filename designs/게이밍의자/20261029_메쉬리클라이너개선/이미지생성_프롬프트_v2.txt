Use case: product-mockup.
Create ONE wide premium industrial design presentation board showing THREE genuinely distinct new designs for a mesh upholstered reclining gaming chair for Korean PC gaming cafes. Label them only 01 / 02 / 03 in small tasteful typography above each column. All three MUST be substantially more resolved, sculptural and contemporary than the customer's current bulky butterfly-wing recliner. Do NOT merely simplify seams or round the old chair. Customer rejected those safe timid redesigns. Design at the level of a leading contemporary industrial design studio.

REFERENCE ROLES:
Image 1: customer prototype ONLY establishes product category, generous seat comfort and practical PC cafe use. Its butterfly upper back and stacked bulky cushions are explicitly REJECTED. Do not preserve that outline.
Image 2: Fractal Refine, take the precise continuous rim, integrated technical architecture and clarity of major masses.
Image 3: Herman Miller Embody, take sophisticated narrow-waist back proportions, subtle transverse support rhythm, and visual lightness; add proper head support for our reclining use.
Image 4: RECARO Exo, take decisive shoulder geometry, architectural transitions, defined shell vs contact padding, NOT logos, same belt holes or bright blue panels.
Image 5: Haworth Fern, take modern dimensional knit surface and continuous body-hugging sculptural volume.

Composition: triptych, three equal-width columns on the same warm off-white studio background with identical elegant neutral lighting. Each column shows ONE large full chair in front three-quarter view, from headrest through its FIVE-spoke dark base and five casters, all fully visible. Consistent natural adult-chair scale and camera angle. Large product renders, little unused whitespace. No other views, inset photos, people, desks, decorations or marketing copy. Crisp premium product photography / photorealistic industrial CAD rendering, very high detail on mesh knit, matte polymer and upholstery. Shadows anchor all wheels to floor. All chairs black/graphite and dark warm gray, barely visible restrained cool gray accent, no chrome and no bright color panels. Black does not mean hide the form: illuminate clear material and geometry transitions. No logos/brands/RGB/diamond quilting/carbon-fiber print/luxury car upholstery. Technical mesh textile is essential on EVERY concept and must be visually legible.

01 — AIRFRAME: closest to premium minimal Fractal/Razer aesthetic. A tall, lean continuous rounded trapezoid outer graphite structural frame around a SINGLE curved tension-mesh upper back, with visible controlled transparency. The perimeter frame bends forward gently at the lumbar, strong elegant S profile, not bulky padded wings. A separate slim curved horizontal headrest with a small deliberate negative-space gap above the mesh back, mounted on a credible rear sliding support. At lower lumbar a narrow contoured upholstered support bridges behind the mesh. Pair breathable back with a generous thick 70-ish-mm-looking foam seat under dense charcoal spacer mesh, flatter wide seat free of raised racing thigh wings. Black adjustable padded armrests, robust concealed reclining joint, clean rounded shell beneath the seat. This is a hybrid mesh-back / foam-seat reclining chair, not all-transparent seat. Strong clean perimeter silhouette. Crisp useful engineering beauty.

02 — ARCH: our most distinctive new gaming design, drawing on RECARO's precision but more mature and futuristic. A single sculptural angular dark graphite rear shell forming a restrained broad shoulder yoke, narrowing elegantly at waist, no butterfly puffed wings. A shallow integrated head pad, ONE slim horizontal ventilation slot underneath the head-support section (not twin belt harness holes), rounded-rectangle corners and clean controlled chamfers. A gently curved elongated inset charcoal fine-mesh-over-foam back contact panel, framed by two slim faceted side rails; no separate fat lumbar pillow. A visually floating lower-back cushion integrated in the shell with a small genuine vertical gap behind it, attached credibly. Broad padded dark mesh seat with a thin graphite lower shell edge. Thick enough for PC cafe reclining comfort but slim-looking edges. Crisp forward-slanted adjustable arm posts and wide soft rectangular arm pads. Side reclining mechanism gets a compact flush round dark cover and a tactile short adjustment lever. Distinctive, instantly recognizable silhouette, not a conventional racing chair.

03 — KNIT LOUNGE: sculptural furniture-meets-gaming, inspired by Fern and Embody but fully supports reclining head. A tall softly tapered one-piece back with subtly concave rounded upper corners, narrow perimeter and no separate chunky bolsters. Dark charcoal engineered 3D knit wraps the back seamlessly, a sophisticated gradient of fine vertical channels and small recessed support cells (NOT diamond quilt stitching and NOT big puffy pillows), clean edges reveal a thin dark warm gray outer shell. A refined small oval head cushion nested onto the top portion, integrated rear mounting, no straps. Back has a graceful narrow waist and slightly broader lumbar below. A generous floating-look thick soft mesh seat with rounded waterfall front, all solidly supported by a visible discreet dark cradle. Two slim straight adjustable arms with pill-shaped soft pads. This concept should look like a high-design lounge seat that happens to be an ergonomic gaming recliner, with soft tactile technical knit and calm sculptural stance.

Practical invariants: adult proportions, comfortable usable seat, reasonable back-to-seat gap, two independent securely mounted armrests per chair, realistic gas lift and five-caster base, plausible steel under-seat recline/tilt hardware. Treat new back frames, headrest interfaces and mechanisms as unvalidated design proposals; do not add dimensions or engineering certification claims. Each of the THREE designs must differ in construction architecture, headrest form, back silhouette and mesh treatment, NOT just seams or colors. Overall impression: desirable contemporary premium consumer products, intentional design, manufacturing-believable.
