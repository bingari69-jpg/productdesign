THE NEW SHAPE: a slender compact conventional oval bristle HEAD on a thin rounded neck, leading into a radically sculptural OPEN RIBBON-LOOP HANDLE. The long handle splits into TWO flowing asymmetrical polymer ribbons, leaving one large elongated almond-shaped OPEN WINDOW between them through most of the handle. Both ribbons reunite smoothly at the rounded lower heel and merge again at the neck, forming a single coherent monolithic body with one airy void. One ribbon sweeps outward gently and twists by approximately a quarter turn as it descends, showing a broad gently concave thumb pad near the upper grip, while the opposite ribbon forms a quieter curved finger-rest spine. Controlled elegant asymmetry, like a single ribbon caught in a precise slow twist, NOT a random pretzel, NOT a straight bar with a drilled slot, NOT a heart-shaped novelty item, NOT a spiral spring, NOT a utensil or razor. The negative space and twisting surfaces are the unmistakable design identity. Smooth closed radiused internal transitions, no inaccessible tiny seams or blind holes. Narrow enough for real adult grip: overall length about 190mm visually, compact 25mm-long head, handle maximum width roughly 22mm and grip section 10–14mm thick in silhouette, graceful neck around 5mm width. These are visual design proportions, not production drawings. The open window occupies middle/lower grip, not the thin neck. The handle is not finger jewelry: do not make a circular finger hole at the top or a very wide loop. No independent moving joints, no electronics, no mechanism and no interchangeable head latch.

Practical shape: softly rounded small bristle head suitable in category for ordinary manual brushing, dense separate SOFT WHITE BRISTLE TUFTS installed perpendicular to head face, bristle length ~10mm in visual proportion, realistic filament detail and tiny rounded tuft ends. It has no blade, no silicone spike head, no foam brush or glowing cleaning light. Back of head is smooth polymer, compact and rounded. The head/front aligns for a plausible grip and brushing orientation, no bizarre perpendicular handle placement. Thumb pad faces same general side as bristle face. Shallow molded microtexture on thumb contact area only, no deep dirt-trapping grooves or large embossed details.

MATERIAL/COLOR: fine satin matte warm ivory main polymer body with a very restrained muted mineral-aqua overmold or soft-touch insert ONLY on the concave thumb pad (small elongated 25mm-looking area) and a matching small inner finger contact patch. No glossy chrome, wood, metallic blades or transparent sci-fi parts. The loop's cut edges read as substantial smooth molded polymer with gentle fillets, fine warm shadows inside open void, realistic thickness and manufacturable-looking cross sections. White bristles, two very pale aqua central tufts only to echo pad. Sculptural premium everyday object, unusually memorable but not grotesque. Think high craft product-design prototype, visually simple from a distance, clever in three dimensions.
